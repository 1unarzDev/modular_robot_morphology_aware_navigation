# predetach_check_v1: declaration

Written and committed before any trial of this design exists. Design
`design.json` (hash `74f83427c7ca5337621cd8fc9b2c37bdca59f1cb0f1a62378430a04d35cb1cfb`, 276 trials); cell-by-cell predictions
`predictions.json`, generated by `modular_robot_benchmarks.predetach_check`
from the planner-level model before any trial; smoke subset
`smoke_trial_ids.json` (10 trials = `run_order` 0..9); how to run
it: `RUNBOOK.md`.

**Status, stated plainly.** This design was motivated by analyses of the
`confirmatory_v3` records of the *same* layouts (review r9; wave 4 and its
sourcing addendum). The same-layout arm is therefore a planned, executed check
of a modelled result on the layouts that suggested it, **not** a fresh-layout
confirmation, and it is reported as exploratory. The fresh-layout arm (10 new
sensing-family layouts, new world and plant seeds, generated by the shipped
generator 3 without selection) is the **confirmatory arm**; its predictions
are fixed here, before any of its trials. Records are never pooled with
`confirmatory_v3`; the same-layout arm reuses replicate-0 seeds on purpose
(paired re-execution), which is why it cannot be pooled.

## Question

`feasibility_coupled` loses every non-neutral sensing-family layout of
`confirmatory_v3` (90/90 trials, `unsafe_topology` after the first docking
refusal). Does a feasibility-only planner with **sensing in the loop and no
prediction model** — check, before detaching, that the station currently sees
every pod the transition will move; otherwise reject the site, exclude a
neighbourhood, replan — recover those missions in the simulator, and how much
of `sensing_feasibility_coupled`'s advantage survives it? Secondary: does the
docked-point probe variant, which tests the station's view of each future
docked position with the still-compact body, recover the cases the check
cannot see?

## Methods (cells per layout)

| method | rule | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `feasibility_coupled` | – | shipped baseline, no recovery (reference) |
| `feasibility_checked` | `ray_wedge`, `radius` | pre-detach check at compact ports; on failure exclude (r = 0.3 m disk, or ±10° wedge behind each unseen pod from the station's declared position), replan from the current pose |
| `feasibility_probed` | `ray_wedge`, `radius` | as checked, and if the compact check passes, stand the compact body at 4 offsets along its heading (±0.12, ±0.48 m) so a pod's fiducial sits on each moved pod's docked point; any unseen docked point rejects the site |
| `sensing_feasibility_coupled` | – | shipped treatment (prediction from the prior map) |

Parameters (navigator, `hybrid_navigator.predetach_*`): dwell 1.0 s (exceeds
the estimator's 0.5 s staleness window; only estimates stamped ≥ 0.5 s after
the dwell starts are read), attempt cap 8 rejections, deadline 300 s (the
observer's). No recovery after a refused docking: the mission aborts as today.
Visibility input: the estimator's `relative_pose_estimate`
(`connector_visible`), nothing else.

## Layouts

- **Same-layout arm (exploratory):** the 30 `confirmatory_v3` layouts where
  `feasibility_coupled` was refused (all 15 non-neutral `docking_observability`
  and 15 non-neutral `combined_constraints`; read from the committed records
  archive, consistent with `failure_table.json`), replicate-0 seeds copied.
- **Controls (6):** `docking_observability-00`, `-04`, `combined_constraints-00`
  (neutral) and `reconfiguration_workspace-01`, `-02`, `-03`; replicate-0 seeds.
  The check must change nothing here.
- **Fresh arm (confirmatory):** `docking_observability-21, -22, -23, -25, -26`
  and `combined_constraints-21, -22, -23, -25, -26`, master seed 20261109,
  seeds disjoint from every frozen design (tested).

One replicate; 276 trials; estimated 14.3 h of wall time at
4.5 min per completed mission, 1.5 min per docking refusal, 0.5 min per
planning failure, +0.25 min per rejection (`predictions.json["estimated_hours"]`).

## Hypotheses

- **H1 (same layouts, exploratory).** `feasibility_checked` completes the
  layouts the model predicts it completes and no others (per rule, table above);
  in particular it does **not** rescue the layouts where the hidden pod is
  visible at its compact port and hidden only when docked (predicted
  `docking_refused` with 0 rejections).
- **H2 (controls).** In all 6 controls every checked/probed cell completes with
  0 rejections, as `feasibility_coupled` does.
- **H3 (fresh arm, confirmatory).** On the 10 fresh layouts the completion count
  of each checked/probed cell equals the model's prediction within ±2 layouts,
  and the paired difference to `sensing_feasibility_coupled` has the predicted
  sign.
- **H4 (probe).** `feasibility_probed` completes at least as many layouts as
  `feasibility_checked` under the same rule.

## Outcomes

- **Primary:** mission completion (`completed` true, terminal `READY`) within
  the 300 s simulated deadline.
- **Secondary:** simulated mission time; refused dockings
  (`reconfiguration_failures`, `unsafe_topology`); number of rejections and
  replans (`controller_diagnostics.predetach_checks`); final transition site.

## What would falsify the planner-level model

- A checked trial whose first observed check verdict (per pod) differs from the
  predicted verdict at a site the model did not flag `fragile` (the
  wave-4 addendum found 11/90 record-vs-nominal disagreements, all of them
  "record hidden, nominal visible", so predicted *passes* on fragile sites
  are the expected failure direction, not a falsification).
- More than 3 of the 30 same-layout `feasibility_checked/ray_wedge` cells, or
  more than 2 of the 10 fresh ones, ending in a different outcome class
  (completed / docking_refused / no_plan / attempt_cap / timeout) than
  predicted, excluding infrastructure failures.
- Any control cell failing, or any reference cell (`feasibility_coupled`)
  departing from its `confirmatory_v3` outcome on the same seeds.

## Stop rules and interim look

- **Smoke (seeds 0..9).** Stop and diagnose before tier 1 if any checked
  smoke trial lacks `predetach_checks`, has a `start` event whose rule differs
  from the spec, or reports `fresh_samples == 0` for every pod at a check (the
  check failing closed for a clock reason); or if a control smoke trial does
  not complete.
- **Interim look after tier 1** (`feasibility_checked` on the 36 same-layout
  layouts, seeds 10..74): score with `--score`. Stop the run if (a) any control
  fails, or (b) more than 6 of the 60 refused-layout tier-1 cells end in a
  different outcome class than predicted (excluding infrastructure), which
  would mean the model is not fit to interpret tiers 2–4. Otherwise continue.
- **Probe safety (tier 3).** The navigator does not test probe poses against
  the map; the model reports, per probed check, whether every probe pose is
  free for the compact footprint (`probe_geometry.all_free`, summarized above)
  but does not penalize a non-free probe. If any probed trial ends in
  `collision`, or a probe alignment fails, during probing (check events with
  `reached: false`), stop tier 3 and diagnose; the probed variant's
  predictions are then void for sites whose probes are not free.
- Infrastructure failures are retried by `mission_batch` (2 retries) and
  reported, never counted as robot outcomes.
- No parameter (dwell, radius, wedge, cap) is changed after the first trial.

Per cell: observed vs predicted outcome class and rejection count; per method
and arm: completions with exact binomial intervals; paired (same seeds)
completion and time differences vs `sensing_feasibility_coupled` and vs

only confirmatory comparison. Results are reported for every cell, including
